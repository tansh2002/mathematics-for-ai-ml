
## What is a Variable?

A **variable** is a symbol or letter used in mathematics to represent a value that can change or vary depending on the scenario [[03:07](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DtHYis-DP0oU%26t%3D187)].

---

### 1. The Real-World Intuition

* **Scenario:** Imagine working at a restaurant where you earn a fixed hourly wage of **$10/hour**, plus whatever you make in tips [[00:00](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DtHYis-DP0oU%26t%3D0)].
* **Expression:**

$$\text{Total Earnings} = 10 + \text{tips}$$


* Because the amount of tips changes from hour to hour (busy lunch vs. slow afternoon), the word **tips** is a **variable** [[00:35](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DtHYis-DP0oU%26t%3D35)].

---

### 2. Evaluating the Expression

When the value of the variable changes, the value of the entire expression changes accordingly [[01:46](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DtHYis-DP0oU%26t%3D106)]:

* **Scenario A (Busy lunch rush):**
* Tips = $\$30$ [[00:51](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DtHYis-DP0oU%26t%3D51)]
* Total = $10 + 30 = \mathbf{\$40}$ [[01:08](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DtHYis-DP0oU%26t%3D68)]


* **Scenario B (Slow hour):**
* Tips = $\$5$ [[01:25](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DtHYis-DP0oU%26t%3D85)]
* Total = $10 + 5 = \mathbf{\$15}$ [[01:39](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DtHYis-DP0oU%26t%3D99)]

### 3. Algebraic Notation

In algebra, full words are rarely written out as variables [[01:54](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DtHYis-DP0oU%26t%3D114)]. Instead:

* **Single Letters:** Letters are typically used as shorthand. For example, replacing $\text{tips}$ with $t$ gives the algebraic expression:

$$10 + t$$



where $t = \text{tips earned in an hour}$ [[02:08](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DtHYis-DP0oU%26t%3D128)].
* **Common Symbols:** You can use letters like $x$ ($10 + x$) or even arbitrary symbols like a star ($\star$), though standard letters like $x$, $y$, and $t$ are standard practice in algebra [[02:40](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.youtube.com%2Fwatch%3Fv%3DtHYis-DP0oU%26t%3D160)].

---

### Summary Checklist for Notes

* **Constant:** A fixed value that does not change (e.g., the $\$10$ base wage).
* **Variable:** A symbol representing a changing quantity (e.g., $t$ for tips).
* **Algebraic Expression:** A combination of constants, variables, and operations (e.g., $10 + t$).
